### Intendend Constraints: User Setup Recommendations

#### Setup Recommendation Scope
*   **Brake Bias**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Percentage (%) of total braking force distributed to the front wheels.
    *   **Possible Values:** 40.0% to 60.0% (adjustable in 0.1% or 0.25% increments depending on the car).

*   **Rear Wing**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Integer position steps determining rear aerodynamic downforce levels.
    *   **Possible Values:** 1 to 12 (discrete position steps varying slightly by manufacturer homologation).

*   **Front Ride Height**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Millimeters (mm) measuring static ground clearance at the front reference point.
    *   **Possible Values:** 45 mm to 85 mm (adjustable in 0.5 mm or 1.0 mm increments).

*   **Rear Ride Height**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Millimeters (mm) measuring static ground clearance at the rear reference point to establish rake.
    *   **Possible Values:** 60 mm to 115 mm (adjustable in 0.5 mm or 1.0 mm increments).

*   **Front Anti-Roll Bars (ARB)**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Discrete stiffness steps/clicks.
    *   **Possible Values:** 1 to 7 (where 1 is fully soft and 7 is fully stiff; max values vary by car).

*   **Rear Anti-Roll Bars (ARB)**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Discrete stiffness steps/clicks.
    *   **Possible Values:** 1 to 7 (where 1 is fully soft and 7 is fully stiff; max values vary by car).

*   **Tire Pressures**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Kilopascals (kPa) for cold inflation pressures.
    *   **Possible Values:** 130 kPa to 160 kPa cold (adjustable in 1 kPa increments; target hot operating pressure is roughly 200 kPa to 210 kPa).

*   **Traction Control Cut (TC Cut)**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Electronic map positions regulating the severity of engine ignition/fuel cuts when wheelspin occurs.
    *   **Possible Values:** 1 to 11 (where 1 is minimum power reduction and 11 is maximum intervention).

*   **Traction Control Slip (TC Slip)**
    *   **Adjustment Type:** Electronic map positions dictating the allowed threshold of rear wheel slip before traction control is triggered.
    *   **Possible Values:** 1 to 11 (where 1 allows the most slip/rotation and 11 allows the least).
